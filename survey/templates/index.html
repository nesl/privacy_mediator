Smart-Space Data Sharing Survey

You will review short smart-space scenarios. In this survey, a smart-space system means a sensor/app system used in a home, rental, workplace, school, care setting, public area, or research living space.

Each scenario describes a setting, a sensing-and-software system, and the kind of data or output that would be shared with an app, software service, or person. Scenario details define the sensing task and then list the setting, person, recipient, purpose, handling condition, and data/output. Some situations may look similar, but the type of data shared may differ. Please rate each one separately.

Give your own judgment from the perspective of the person the data is about, or someone responsible for that person. You are not being asked whether you would buy the system, whether the system is technically useful, or whether society as a whole would approve.
