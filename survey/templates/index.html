Smart-Space Data Sharing Survey

You will review short smart-space scenarios. In this survey, a smart-space system means a monitoring device used in a home, rental, workplace, school, care setting, public area, or research living space.

Some questions may belong to the same general scenario, such as a particular task in a particular setting, but show different details. When a row is marked Changed, it means that row differs from the previous question. For each question, rate the version shown on that page as its own complete situation.

Your task is to rate how appropriate it is for the listed data to be shared in each version. Assume that only the listed data is saved or shared, and that only the named receiver gets it unless another party is explicitly stated.

Give your own personal judgment from the perspective of the person the data is about.
