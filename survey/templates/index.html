Smart-Space Data Sharing Survey

You will review short smart-space scenarios. In this survey, a smart-space system means a sensing system used in a home, rental, workplace, school, care setting, public area, or research living space.

Each scenario describes a setting, a sensing system, and the kind of data or output that would be shared with an app, software service, or person. Scenario details list what data is shared, where sensing happens, who the data is about, who receives the output, why it is shared, and any notice, consent, access, or processing condition. Some situations may look similar, but the type of data shared may differ.

Your task is to rate how appropriate it is for the listed data or output to be shared in each situation. Please rate each scenario separately.

Give your own personal judgment from the perspective of the person the data is about, or someone responsible for that person.

This study was reviewed by the UCLA Institutional Review Board and certified as exempt from IRB review under 45 CFR 46.104(d)(2) (Exempt Category 2).
