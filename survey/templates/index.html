Smart-Space Data Sharing Survey

What this survey is about

You will review short scenarios about smart spaces. A smart space is a home, building, rental property, workplace, care setting, public area, or research living space that has sensors and software installed in it.

For example, a smart home or building might use cameras, microphones, motion sensors, door sensors, wearable or room sensors, or other connected devices to detect what is happening in the space. These systems might try to detect things like visitors, falls, daily activities, occupancy, alarms, loud sounds, or other safety-relevant events.

The scenarios ask whether it is appropriate for a monitoring device in that smart space to share a particular kind of data, such as camera images, audio, stick-figure body outlines, or video with sound.

What you will do

  • For each page, rate how appropriate it is for the listed data to be shared in that situation.
  • Assume that only the listed data is saved or shared, and that only the named receiver gets it unless another party is explicitly stated.
  • Give your own personal judgment from the perspective of the person the data is about.
  • Some questions may belong to the same general scenario but show different details. When a row is marked Changed, it means that row differs from the previous question. Please rate each page as its own complete situation.
